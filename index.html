Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does AI Visibility Auditing evaluate web pages? +

Our crawler checks DOM elements, headings hierarchy, OpenGraph metadata, Schema.org JSON-LD, crawlability, response time, and semantic content to produce actionable grades and recommendations.

How to achieve an 80% to 90%+ score on external website checkers? +

Implement valid semantic HTML5 landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), optimal 50-65 character title tags, 130-160 character meta descriptions, a single H1 heading, canonical link, complete OpenGraph and Twitter cards, valid robots.txt and sitemap.xml, and Schema.org structured data.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)? +

AEO optimizes content so that AI engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Copilot can extract concise, factual direct answers from structured Q&A formats, clear heading hierarchies, and FAQPage Schema markup.
